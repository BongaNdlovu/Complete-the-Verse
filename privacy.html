Privacy

Complete the Verse is a Scripture memory game. You can play it entirely on this device with no account.

On this device

Progress, settings, and a short diagnostics buffer are stored in browser storage on this device. Clearing site data erases the local record. A failed save is copied to a backup key before a fresh record is started.

Optional cloud

Cloud sync is optional. If you sign in with a magic link, Supabase stores your email, a display name you choose, and a copy of your save so progress can follow you across devices.

Leaderboards

Daily and Blitz boards show display name, score, accuracy, and difficulty. They do not show your email. Scores are accepted only through the trusted submit-score function.

Processors

The game is hosted on Vercel. Optional accounts, saves, and boards use Supabase. Live map weather uses Open-Meteo. Map tiles may be requested from Esri or OpenStreetMap when you open the atlas online.

Deletion

Sign out from Settings to stop syncing this device. Use Erase all progress to clear the local record. To delete a cloud account and its rows, email the operator from the address you signed in with and ask for erasure.
