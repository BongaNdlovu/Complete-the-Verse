# Complete the Verse

This context defines the shared language for the pilgrimage gameplay and its visual direction.

## Journey

**Patriarchs Journey**:
The first pilgrimage arc, beginning at Ur and following Abraham's road through the patriarchal sites.
_Avoid_: Patriarchs mode, Abraham level

**Abraham**:
The named biblical pilgrim represented by the supplied traveler illustration in the Patriarchs Journey.
_Avoid_: generic traveler

**Site**:
A named destination on a journey, such as Ur or Haran, where a sequence of verses is played.
_Avoid_: level, stage

## Gameplay surface

**Question Screen**:
The reusable live gameplay surface where the verse, active answer mechanic, timer, progress, and journey-specific site identity appear together. Its composition adapts to the density of every current mechanic.
_Avoid_: quiz screen, trial screen

**Mechanic Variant**:
One of the existing ways a Question Screen asks for recall, including choices, typed recall, cloze, duels, and passages.
_Avoid_: mini-game, mode screen

## Visual direction

**Video Backdrop**:
Existing site footage shown behind the Question Screen, remaining visible around the interface while localized contrast treatment protects readability. Ur keeps its own video; later sites use their own still or video treatment rather than reusing Ur footage.
_Avoid_: background video

**Character Illustration**:
The large Abraham artwork accompanying active Patriarchs Question Screens, positioned beside the interface with subtle motion rather than a separate animated character system. It is a separate visual from the Video Backdrop, adapts to mechanic density, and is hidden behind full-screen overlays or when space is tight.
_Avoid_: character sprite, avatar
