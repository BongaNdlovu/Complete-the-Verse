How Complete
the Verse works

A quiz on the King James Bible. One phrase is missing. You finish it before the clock runs out. This page is a map of what happens when you play.

How much time you get

Later places are tighter. A hot streak gives a little more. Typing always gets a long clock.

Start of the roadAbout 24 seconds to pick
→
End of the roadAbout 15 seconds to pick
Three right in a rowThe next pick clock grows by about a fifth. That starts at “Building.”
The swift verseOne shorter question in the middle of each city. Still long enough to read.
When you typeAbout half a minute. The last two verses of every city, and Recall.
Special roundsRapid Recall and the like use their own clocks. They do not steal the extra time.

The rest of the hall

Who you areEight scholars. You pick one face. That token sits on the map and on the verse stage. Biblical heroes are not skins.
RelicsClear a city for the first time and you recover one object from that place. They live in a cabinet, not a 3D vault.
SealsBadges for first run, long streaks, finishing an arc, walking without rest.
StudyVerses you missed, waiting to be learned again.
MusicThe slider to zero means silence. The game used to leak a whisper. That is fixed.
What this is notNot 3D. Not a film you cannot read. The verse always stays readable.
