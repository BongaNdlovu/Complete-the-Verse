# Complete the Verse — Product plan

A King James pilgrimage you open onto a live verse, walk one site in the time it takes to drink a coffee, type one line from memory, and come back tomorrow because the road — not a menu — is waiting.

The quality bar is: I would pay **$8–12 once**, without hesitation. Price is not the work. This feeling is.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Status:** locked by the maker after two grill rounds. Life after Patmos is the spiral. Streak celebration and the seven-day reward are locked below.

---

## The score

The 30-day score is a **habit they do not hate**. Verses are what the habit leaves in the body.

- If they open most days and still cannot produce a single line, we failed the floor.
- If they can recite and they quit in a week, we failed the product.

## After the 46th site: the spiral

Tomorrow is the next pin on the **same road**, at a colder standard. Not a new place. Not a quiz list.

- First walk: recognize, then one prove-it.
- Second walk: more of the site is assembled from memory.
- Third walk: colder still.

**Why not “today’s station”?** That is Daily with a costume. Daily is dead. A generated stop is not Ur, not Sinai, not the book.

**Why not “pick a site to re-walk”?** That is a menu. Menus are forbidden in the first 45 seconds. Guilty starters will pick easy Ur forever and the ordeal dies.

The spiral keeps the map honest, the coffee the same length, the ordeal climbing, and the app able to throw them onto a verse with no asking.

**During the first 46 days**, review still happens: one or two **due** verses from earlier pins are folded into today’s eight. They replace the generic bank-fill verses, not extra homework on top. Yesterday’s miss can walk into Haran. That is pressure + return without a Drill.

## The contradiction, settled in writing

Watchman energy **and** a guilty starter who has quit every Bible app.

Mercy is **not** easier questions, more lamps, or a “Disciple” pillow.

Mercy is three things only:

1. **The ordeal is recall, not reading speed.** A long KJV sentence at Ur must be finishable by the eye. The clock is *read time + think time*. Think time shrinks as you go east. We never fail someone who was still reading.
2. **Death is cheap.** Two lamps stay. Fail a site and you lose the attempt, not the verses, not the day, not your face. One tap, same site, same ritual.
3. **The miss comes back.** Same coffee if a slot remains; otherwise the next time that pin (or tomorrow’s fold-in) can hold it. A failed verse is not allowed to vanish.

Early-clock honesty goes no further than “time to read.” After the Patriarchs, the think window starts closing. By Sinai the road is the road.

## Learning that is not homework and not trivia

**Not strenuous**

- One coffee. Then the day continues.
- Most of the site is recognition (options). That is how a starter can play.
- No Study Hall, no “15 due,” no tutorial wall, no profile gate before the first verse.
- After the coffee: relic, one line, done.

**Not cheap**

- The last beat of every coffee is **produce it** — typed or assembled, no options. If they only tap A–D forever, the ritual is empty and we do not ship it.
- A miss returns. The road remembers.
- East tightens. Second pass is colder.
- The verse is bound to a place. They are not memorizing a flashcard; they left a line at a well, a mountain, an island.

**What we owe a life, not an exam**

After 30 days we do not need a recitation exam. We need: they still open it, they have produced dozens of KJV lines under a clock, and a handful of those lines come back when the week is hard. The map is how the line has a body. The return is how it stays. The seven-day streak is how the habit is *seen* — not how we grade their soul.

We do **not** write 56 book-commentary cards. That is seminary. One sentence of the place (the quote already on the pin) is enough teaching. The verse does the rest.

## The weekday coffee (target: 8–12 minutes)

**8 verses. Not 9. Not a finale stacked on top that blows the clock.**

| Beat | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Recognition, clock honest to the words | They can play. Streaks and pressure live here. |
| 1 of those 5 | Yesterday’s miss, if one is due | Return happens *inside* the coffee |
| 1 | A swift / shift so the body is not five identical taps | Variety without a new mode |
| 1 | Set-piece only at the sites that already deserve a climax | A finale every stop stops being a finale |
| **Last** | **Produce one verse. No options.** | The floor. Non-negotiable. |

If they fail the prove-it, the site may still complete (habit over shame) but that verse is due. Clearing is not the same as being done with the line.

**After the last verse:** relic if earned, streak tick (see below), one spoken line, map pulse on the pin. The close stays under 20 seconds unless today is a celebration day (3 / 7 / 14 / 30), in which case the cinematic may hold a few seconds more. Then the app can sit. Next launch is the next verse.

Eight verses in 8–12 minutes only works if correct answers keep chaining and the close is short. Two typed finales + a set piece + a results essay will make this a 20-minute class. We will not do that.

## Streaks — celebrate them, don’t tax them

There are two streaks. They must never be confused on screen.

### 1. In-run combo (inside the coffee)

The momentum steps the game already owns: **3 / 5 / 8 / 12**. These are the fire in a single site.

Today they are mostly a badge and a meter. That is not a celebration. Each step needs its own **animation and asset** — a stamp, a lamp flare, a sound, a brief full-screen mark — climbing to Overdrive at 12. A player should *see and hear* they are at ×4 without reading the HUD.

This does not change the ordeal. It makes the ordeal loud. Miss still collapses the combo. That collapse should also be visible: the fire goes out, not just a number resetting.

### 2. Habit streak (across days)

Consecutive **calendar days on which they finished a coffee** — not days they merely opened the app. Opening is cheap. Completing the prove-it coffee is the ritual.

- The count lives on the **close** of the coffee and on the map after, never on the first 45 seconds.
- Days 1 and 2: a small lamp lights. Enough to notice. Not a ceremony.
- Day 3: first celebration — a short mark, a voice line, a lamp that stays lit.
- **Day 7: the reward.** See below.
- Days 14 and 30: the same language, larger. They are echoes of seven, not a new economy.

A broken streak **resets the count only**. It does not take relics, verses, lamps, seals, or the road. No shame screen. No “you lost your week.” No streak-freeze to buy back. A guilty starter who misses Tuesday must be able to start Wednesday without being punished for being the person this game is for.

### The seven-day reward

Finish a coffee on seven consecutive days.

**They receive:**

- A unique **seal** that cannot be earned any other way (working name: *The Seventh Lamp*).
- A dedicated **cinematic**: seven lamps, the seventh catching. Original art and a voice line. This is a real asset, not a CSS flash on the score.
- The seal sits in the cabinet. The lamps stay visible on the map as a quiet record.

**They do not receive:**

- A third life, a slower clock, a skipped prove-it, coins, or a verse unlock.
- Anything that makes day 8 easier than day 7. The gift is honour, not a pillow.

Seven is the first time the habit is *crowned*. If day 7 feels like a settings toast, we failed the feature.

### Assets this requires

| Asset | For |
|---|---|
| Combo stamps / flares at 3, 5, 8, 12 (and the miss collapse) | In-run |
| Overdrive entrance that is an event, not a class toggle | In-run |
| Small lamp-lighting for habit days 1–6 | Habit |
| Seventh-lamp cinematic + seal art + voice line | Day 7 |
| Larger echoes for 14 and 30 (can reuse the 7 language, scaled) | Habit |

Do not ship the seven-day *count* without the seven-day *gift*. A counter with no crown is Duolingo without the owl — just guilt.

## What must change in the game that exists

Grounded in the tree as of 2026-08-18:

**The front door.** `MENU_ORDER` in `js/briefs.js` still leads with pilgrimage **and** daily, blitz, trial, endless, practice. Boot still walks intro → hall. Atlas still has its own cold-open card. Site brief is still a screen between map and verse. All of that fights “already on a verse.”

**The coffee length.** A site is already 8 verses, last two typed, plus optional set piece, plus brief, plus results. That is a class. Keep 8, drop to **one** required production beat, and starve everything around it.

**The clock.** `CLOCK_OPEN` 14s / `CLOCK_CLOSE` 6.5s then gets pad + 20% pace + 5s flat, so Ur is already ~21s and Patmos ~13s on picks. The lie is not “too few seconds at Ur.” The lie is a 40-word sentence on a clock that does not care how long the sentence is. **Scale think-time to the road; scale read-time to the verse.**

**Death / bank.** Verses commit when served, not when the run starts — that is already the right mercy. Keep it. Do not burn a quit. Do not lock a day (there is no Daily).

**Difficulty.** Watchman stays the only ordeal (2 lamps). Do not add Disciple as a pillow. Early **read** time is the on-ramp.

**Review.** SRS already writes every answer. The bug is product: The Drill is the only thing that *serves* due verses, and The Drill is dying. Due verses must be drawn **into** `drawSite` as reserved slots. If that is not wired, the learning model is a speech.

**Post-Patmos.** `isUnlocked` is linear and stops. The spiral is a second standard on the same 46 ids, auto-chosen at launch. Pilgrim’s Recall already exists as a hidden full-typed site — that is the second-pass engine, not a menu item.

**Streaks.** In-run momentum already steps at 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 and Overdrive already exists as a choice. What is missing is the celebration layer (animation, assets, miss collapse) and any *habit* streak at all. There is no consecutive-day coffee count, no seventh-day seal, no lamp cinematic. Those have to be built. Do not hang them off Daily — Daily is dead. The unit is “coffee finished today.”

## Build order

Each phase should be playable alone. Do not start 4 before 1 is true on a phone.

### 1 — The front door

Launch is a verse. First-run tap lands on Ur’s first blank. Returning launch lands on the next coffee’s first blank. Modes gone from the hall. Site brief is not in the way.

**Done when:** a stranger is answering in under 45 seconds.

### 2 — The coffee

One site, 8–12 minutes, one prove-it, close in 20 seconds. Clock is read + shrinking think. Death is cheap. Miss can return in the same eight.

Habit streak is real in this phase: finishing a coffee ticks the day, the close shows the lamp, **day 7 grants the seal and plays the seventh-lamp cinematic**. A first-pass of that cinematic is required here — a counter with no crown is not done. In-run combo can still be the existing meter until phase 5.

**Done when:** you can clear Ur on a weekday without looking at the clock on the wall and feeling robbed, and a seven-day run of coffees actually *lands* as a reward.

### 3 — Honest return

`drawSite` reserves 1–2 slots for due verses from earlier pins. Fail the prove-it → that id is due. No Drill door.

**Done when:** miss at Ur, play Haran, the line can show up. If it never does, we built a quiz.

### 4 — The spiral

After Patmos (and any day with no new pin), launch the next site at the next standard. Map still shows 46. No new mode name.

**Done when:** day 47 is still a coffee, still a place, colder.

### 5 — Only then, juice

Overdrive as a bet, relic armor, set-piece spikes, traveler on the road. In-run combo **assets**: stamps, flares, Overdrive entrance, visible miss collapse. Polish the seventh-lamp cinematic and the 14 / 30 echoes. Not before the front door and the coffee are true. Spectacle on a hall-of-modes quiz is why the game is not worth money. Spectacle on a ritual they already keep — including a week they actually walked — is why it is.

## What we will not do

- Paywall verses, or charge a subscription for Scripture.
- Add NIV/ESV “to grow the market.”
- Soften questions, add a third life, or brand an Easy mode.
- Keep Daily “just for habit.” The spiral *is* the habit.
- Write a commentary product. Place + verse + return is the teacher.
- Turn every site into a set piece.
- Measure success by verses recited at a party. Habit is the score; production is the floor.
- Punish a broken habit streak. Reset the count; take nothing else. No streak-freeze to buy back.
- Reward seven days with an easier road (extra life, slower clock, skipped prove-it). The gift is honour.
- Show the habit streak before the first verse. The count belongs to the close, not the front door.
- Ship a day-count with no day-7 cinematic and seal.

## How we know it worked

**Week 1 (guilty starter):** Opened 5 of 7 days. Finished the coffee more often than they abandoned it. Did not have to ask what to tap. If they finish all seven, the seventh lamp plays and they have the seal.

**Day 30:** Still opening. Have walked a real stretch of the road (or begun the second pass). Have produced a verse with no options on most of those days. A broken week did not make them delete the app.

**Money test:** Sit down, already in it, ten minutes later a pin and a line typed, tomorrow the next one is wanted. Then $8–12 is obvious.

If week 1 fails, do not add features. Fix the front door or the coffee length. Those are the only two things a guilty starter feels.
