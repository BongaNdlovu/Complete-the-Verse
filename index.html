How the trial works
  1. Read the verse — one phrase is taken away.
  2. Tap a phrase (or press A–D / 1–4) — the tap is the answer; it locks at once.
  3. Lifelines: S = Selah (+5s), I = Illuminate (−2 wrong).

The Drill brings back verses on the day they fall due. Recall asks you to type them out with no options at all. Esc pauses any run.
